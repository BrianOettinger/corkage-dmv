BYOB & Corkage Restaurants in Washington, DC

DC won't hand you a $10 corkage, but it will let you pour your own bottle at a Michelin-starred counter. Here's where, and what it costs.

BYOB in DC means corkage at restaurants with full wine programs, not the bring-anything-to-the-noodle-shop scene of other cities. Fees run higher than in suburban Maryland, but the rooms are some of the best in the region. Below are 14 DC restaurants that allow outside wine, grouped by the kind of night you're planning. Fees marked "call to confirm" come from published listings and haven't been phone-verified by us yet, so call before you go. And never bring a bottle that's already on their list.
