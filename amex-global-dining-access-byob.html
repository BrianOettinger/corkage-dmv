The Smart Diner's Guide

Get the Table with Amex Global Dining Access. Bring Your Own Bottle.

Your Amex card can get you into DC's hardest reservations. A corkage policy lets you pour the bottle you've been saving. Here's how to do both.

The hardest part of a great dinner in DC usually isn't the bill. It's getting the reservation. If you carry an American Express Platinum Card, there's a benefit built for exactly that, and at several of the restaurants it covers, you can also bring your own wine.

What Is Global Dining Access?

Global Dining Access by Resy is a benefit for eligible American Express Card Members. It costs nothing extra, and it works through the Resy app and Resy.com:

  • Tables held just for Card Members. Participating restaurants set aside reservations, often at prime times, that only Global Dining Access members can book. They show up as purple time slots in Resy.
  • Priority Notify. When a fully booked restaurant has a table open up, members are alerted first.
  • Special events with chefs and restaurants, available only to members.

Eligible cards include The Platinum Card®, the Business Platinum Card®, the Delta SkyMiles® Reserve Card, and the Hilton Honors Aspire Card, among other premium Amex cards. To turn it on, add your eligible card to your Resy profile. That's it.

The Triple Stack: Table, Food, Wine

Global Dining Access is one of three separate savings you can combine at the same dinner:

1
The table
Global Dining Access gets you a reservation that the general public can't book.
2
The food
The Platinum Card's Resy credit returns up to $100 a quarter, if the restaurant shows "Qualifies for Resy Credit."
3
The wine
Bring your own bottle, pay corkage, and skip the 200–300% restaurant markup.

The two Amex benefits are independent: a restaurant can be part of Global Dining Access without qualifying for the Resy credit, and vice versa. Check each restaurant's Resy page for both.

How to Do It, Step by Step

  1. Add your card to Resy. In the Resy app or on Resy.com, add your eligible Amex card to your profile. This unlocks Global Dining Access.
  2. Look for purple time slots. On a participating restaurant's page, purple times are held for Card Members. If nothing's open, tap Notify, since members hear first.
  3. Check for the Resy credit. Scroll down the restaurant's Resy page for "Qualifies for Resy Credit" if you also want the $100 quarterly credit.
  4. Call about corkage. Confirm the fee and any bottle limit, and make sure your bottle isn't on their list. Our DC BYOB guide has the fees we track.
  5. Pay with your Platinum Card. Food and corkage on the card count toward the Resy credit at qualifying restaurants.
