What Does Corkage Cost in the DMV?

We tracked the corkage fee at every restaurant in our directory. Here's what DC and Montgomery County charge to open your bottle, and where you'll pay the least.

Corkage is the fee a restaurant charges to open and serve a bottle of wine you bring yourself. Across the 35 DC and Montgomery County restaurants in our directory that allow it, corkage runs from $10 to $100 a bottle. The most common fee is $25, and the median is $35.

Montgomery County vs. DC

Where you eat matters more than anything else. Montgomery County's BYOB scene is full of neighborhood spots and suburban steakhouses, so $25 is the norm. DC's list leans toward big-night steakhouses and tasting menus, and the median fee is nearly double.

Is Bringing Your Own Bottle Worth It?

Usually, yes. Restaurants commonly price wine at two to three times what it costs in a shop. Bring a $40 bottle to a restaurant with $25 corkage and you've spent $65, where the same wine on a list could run $80 to $120. The bigger the bottle, the bigger the savings, which is why corkage makes the most sense for wine you already own and care about.

It's a closer call at the top of the range. At $75 or $100 corkage, bring something that's genuinely special or hard to find, not an everyday bottle.

Ask About These When You Call
  • Bottle limits. Aventino Cucina, Sushi Nakazawa, and The Bazaar cap you at two bottles.
  • Large formats. Morton's charges $55 for a magnum instead of $35.
  • Waived bottles. Founding Farmers Potomac waives corkage on your first bottle and charges $25 after that.
  • The list rule. Don't bring a wine the restaurant already sells. In Montgomery County, that's a rule, not just etiquette.
  • Ordering food. Corkage is for diners. Plan to order a meal.

Corkage FAQ

What is corkage?

Corkage is the fee a restaurant charges to open and serve a bottle of wine you bring yourself. It covers the glassware, service, and the wine sales the restaurant gives up. It's usually charged per bottle.

How much is a typical corkage fee in the DC area?

Among the 35 restaurants we track, corkage runs from $10 to $100 per bottle. The most common fee is $25, and the median is $35.

Is corkage more expensive in DC than in Maryland?

Yes. The median among our DC restaurants is $42.50, compared with $25 in Montgomery County. DC's list skews toward steakhouses and tasting menus, where $60 to $100 is common.

Are there restaurants with free corkage?

Rarely. Founding Farmers Potomac waives corkage on your first bottle and charges $25 for each one after. Otherwise the lowest fee we track is $10, at Siriwan Thai in Silver Spring.

Should you tip on corkage?

Yes. The corkage fee goes to the restaurant, but your server still opens and pours your wine. Tip as if you'd ordered a comparable bottle from the list.

Can you bring a wine that's on the restaurant's list?

Generally no. Restaurants that allow outside wine almost always ask that it not appear on their own list, and in Montgomery County that's a rule. Call ahead if you're unsure.
